2. What Is Vietnam LCL Import?

LCL (Less than Container Load) is a shipping method where cargo too small to fill a full container is consolidated with other shippers' cargo and shipped together.

Here are the most common reasons buyers importing from Vietnam choose LCL.

1
Order volume is too small to fill an FCL
FCL (a full dedicated container) is only economical from around 15 CBM. For smaller volumes, LCL is the more sensible choice.
2
You want to test a sample or small trial order first
Many buyers use LCL to receive a small quantity first while vetting a new supplier.
3
Short reorder cycles mean you want to minimize inventory
As a rule of thumb, LCL is economical for roughly 1–15 CBM. Above that, it's worth comparing against FCL costs.

4. Ho Chi Minh vs. Haiphong — Cost and Lead Time Depend on Your Departure Port

There are two main departure ports when importing from Vietnam. Freight rates and lead times differ depending on whether your factory is in the south (near Ho Chi Minh) or the north (near Hanoi/Haiphong).

6. Cut Your Tariffs with the Korea-Vietnam FTA

Using the Korea-Vietnam FTA (VKFTA) can reduce duties to 0% on many items. You need to confirm whether a Certificate of Origin (CO) can be issued before shipping — it's difficult to apply retroactively after arrival. For details on Form KV/RCEP and other CO types and issuing authorities, see our Complete Guide to Vietnam Export & Import.
