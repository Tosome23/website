Korea-Vietnam Shipping Guide 2026
Export & Import, Sea Freight, and Customs

Vietnam is one of Korea's core trade partners. The Korea-Vietnam FTA (VKFTA) offers substantial tariff savings, but overlooking labeling rules and certification requirements is a common way to get stuck at customs. Here's a 2026 reference covering Korea-Vietnam export and import methods, lead times to Ho Chi Minh City and Haiphong, the LCL import process, customs precautions, and how to make the most of the FTA.

1. Korea-Vietnam Trade Overview — Key Categories and FTA Use

Vietnam is one of Korea's top three export destinations. Major categories exported to Vietnam include electrical/electronic components, machinery and equipment, plastic and chemical raw materials, and textile/apparel fabric. In the other direction, major categories imported from Vietnam include finished apparel and footwear, furniture and wood products, seafood, and finished electronics.

The Korea-Vietnam FTA (VKFTA) is a bilateral agreement that took effect in 2015 and phases in tariff reductions on Vietnamese-origin imports. Using a Certificate of Origin (C/O Form KV), many items qualify for tariff rates of 0-5%.

✅ Beyond the Korea-Vietnam FTA, RCEP (Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership) has been in effect since 2022, providing additional tariff benefits for trade with Vietnam. Depending on the item, you can choose whichever of the two FTAs is more favorable.

5. Vietnam Customs Precautions

Vietnam has strict labeling rules and certification requirements. Confirm the following before exporting.

①
Mandatory Vietnamese-language labeling
Almost all consumer goods exported to Vietnam require Vietnamese-language labels. Product name, origin, manufacturer, ingredients, usage instructions, and expiration date must all be labeled in Vietnamese. Missing labels result in a customs hold or return shipment.
②
Food & cosmetics — pre-certification required
Food exported to Vietnam sometimes requires import approval from Vietnam's Ministry of Health. Cosmetics require a Notification filing before sale. These processes can take months, so early preparation is essential.
③
Importing into Korea — check labeling & ingredients
Food and cosmetics imported from Vietnam into Korea need Korean-language labels and ingredient lists meeting MFDS standards. Electronics subject to KC certification cannot be imported at all without a Korean certificate.
④
Dangerous goods — confirm air-freight restrictions
Products with built-in lithium batteries, flammable liquids, and chemicals must comply with IATA regulations for air import/export. Sea freight also requires a separate dangerous-goods declaration (IMDG Code), so coordinate with your forwarder in advance.

6. How to Use Korea-Vietnam FTA Tariff Benefits

Using the Korea-Vietnam FTA properly can significantly reduce your tariff burden. The key is a pre-issued Certificate of Origin (C/O).

CategoryDetails
Applicable FTAVKFTA (Korea-Vietnam FTA, in effect since 2015) / RCEP (in effect since 2022)
C/O formKorea-Vietnam FTA: Form KV / RCEP: Form RCEP
Issuing authorityVietnam: Ministry of Industry and Trade (VCCI) or local chambers of commerce / Korea: Korea Chamber of Commerce and Industry
Submission timingSubmitted to customs at time of import declaration (original or electronic C/O)
NoteC/O is valid for 1 year from date of issue. General tariff applies once expired
⚠️ To claim FTA benefits, you must submit the C/O at the time of import declaration. Late submission after arrival can sometimes be applied retroactively, but the process is complicated. The safest approach is completing C/O issuance before shipping.

7. Common Problems in Korea-Vietnam Export & Import

  • Missing FTA benefits from no C/O — Many shippers miss out on tariff savings by failing to issue a Certificate of Origin or missing the submission window.
  • Missing Vietnamese-language labeling — Consumer goods exported to Vietnam without Vietnamese labels get held at local customs.
  • Incorrect HS code — Vietnam enforces HS code compliance strictly; errors trigger additional taxation or inspection.
  • Missed CFS cutoff — LCL cargo that misses the CFS intake cutoff gets pushed to the next sailing, sometimes delaying shipment by 2+ weeks.
  • Missing food/cosmetics pre-certification — Shipping without Vietnam import approval or Notification registration can result in extended customs-warehouse detention on arrival.
