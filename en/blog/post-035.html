Stocking Korean Skincare in Australia?
Three Checks, Then We Ship It from Korea

Australian retailers and distributors can import Korean cosmetics with a few checks first: AICIS registration, English ingredient labels and sunscreen rules. After that, we handle the Korea side and ship by express, air or ocean. Fragrance and sprays included.

Before your first order: three checks

Australia has no product-by-product approval for cosmetics. Instead, there are three things you, the importer, need in place. Once they are sorted, we handle the Korea side: packing, paperwork and shipping.

1. AICIS registration: if you import cosmetics to sell, register your business with AICIS before the first shipment. There is no minimum quantity, and it renews every year by 31 August.

2. Ingredient labels: retail products need ingredients listed in English or INCI names, in descending order.

3. Sunscreen: products mainly for sun protection are TGA medicines and must be on the ARTG. Foundations and lip products with SPF are generally cosmetics.

Case 2. A 16.5 kg carton of cosmetics, then repeat shipments

In March 2026 we sent one carton of cosmetics and household goods (16.5 kg) to Australia by FedEx. The same customer then shipped massage devices (17 kg each) through us in May and July. Once a first shipment arrives smoothly, most customers keep the same route.

A typical request: weekly shipments that keep growing

A Korean cosmetics brand with a registered Australian importer asked us: "We will ship 25 to 48 kg every week, rising to 250 to 450 kg per shipment in a few months. What are the fixed clearance costs, rates by weight band, and can you pick up?" We plan these in two phases:

  • Now (tens of kg a week): weekly express keeps timing and cost steady.
  • Later (hundreds of kg per shipment): air cargo lowers the cost per kg, and ocean LCL is worth comparing if timing allows.
  • Throughout: pickup in Korea, invoice and packing list, and Australian clearance stay on one process.

The AUD 1,000 line: combine orders or split them?

Consignment valueAt the borderDeclaration
AUD 1,000 or lessNo duty or GST at the border (GST may be charged at sale by a registered seller)Self-assessed clearance
Over AUD 1,000Duty, 10% GST and an import processing chargeFull import declaration

The threshold applies per consignment. If several customer orders travel as one consignment, their values can add up past AUD 1,000. Whether to split or combine is a trade-off between freight and tax, and we work it out with you when quoting.

Korean-origin goods can qualify for lower duty under the Korea-Australia FTA (KAFTA). Under AUD 1,000, no certificate of origin is needed.

Australian customs notes

1. Importer registration: AICIS registration comes first for goods you will sell.

2. Sunscreen: products mainly for sun protection need TGA listing before sale.

3. Invoice: declare real names and values. Lowering values to fit under AUD 1,000 risks penalties.

4. Origin: prepare origin evidence in advance to claim KAFTA rates.

Rules and rates change often. We re-check them when we quote.
