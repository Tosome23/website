2. Is LCL always cheaper for furniture and tile?

Looking at CBM (volume) alone makes LCL look cheaper, but cargo with damage risk like furniture and tile needs to be judged by a different standard.

Even when LCL looks cheaper by CBM alone, FCL is often the safer choice once you factor in potential claim costs from damage.

3. Why do missing fumigation marks on wooden crates block customs clearance?

Wooden packing material (wooden crates) can be turned back at customs without an IPPC (International Plant Protection Convention) fumigation mark. This comes up especially often with cargo like furniture and tile that needs wooden packing.

Tosome Logis handles everything from confirming this mark to designing reinforced, damage-prevention packing. We advise on the right packing method for your cargo type upfront.
