1. What should you check first for Dongguan LCL FCL imports?

The key variable in Dongguan LCL FCL imports is whether your cargo comes from one factory or several. Dongguan (东莞) sits directly north of Shenzhen and is dense with small and mid-size factories making electronics components, small appliances and toys. Even a single finished product often has parts sourced from multiple factories, so it's common to have more than one pickup point.

More pickup points means more complexity in both the forwarding method and schedule management. With a single factory, you can simply choose LCL or FCL based on volume — but with multiple factories, you also need to calculate pickup order and cutoff dates together.

2. How do you consolidate a container when cargo comes from multiple factories?

Combining cargo from multiple factories into one container (FCL) or one console (mixed LCL) is called multi-pickup consolidation. Its advantage is that even with more pickup points, everything can still arrive at one confirmed date.

FactorSingle-factory pickupMulti-pickup consolidation
Pickup points12 or more
Schedule managementOnly that factory's completion dateBased on whichever factory finishes latest
Export methodIndividually exported and shippedConsolidated at one point, exported as one container/console
RiskRelatively lowA delay at one factory affects the whole schedule

If production completion dates differ across factories, the whole shipment can be delayed to match whichever factory finishes latest — so advance coordination matters.

3. How do you coordinate when factories finish production on different schedules?

Tosome Logis checks each factory's production completion date in advance, then coordinates pickup order and container cutoff dates so everything can be exported and consolidated in one pass. Just tell us where your factories are, and we handle the rest.

We work backward from whichever factory finishes latest, holding cargo from earlier-ready factories in temporary storage and picking up in sequence.

4. Problems that come up handling Dongguan LCL FCL imports yourself

When a production delay at one factory affects the whole schedule

A common problem for those coordinating multiple Dongguan factories directly is that a production delay at one factory pushes back the entire shipment schedule. If you set your pickup schedule without checking each factory's completion date in advance, this kind of delay tends to repeat.

When using a different forwarder per factory leads to duplicate freight charges

Some end up being double-charged for freight after using separate forwarders per factory. Without managing pickup schedules and cutoff dates as one unit, this kind of loss happens.
