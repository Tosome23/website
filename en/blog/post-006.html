Direct DHL Contracts Save 40% — Agents Save Up to 80%

Many shippers assume that booking directly through DHL's official website is the cheapest option. In practice, going through an agent is often far more cost-effective. Courier companies like DHL and FedEx offer bulk-contract pricing to large agents who ship high volumes on their behalf — and those agents pass that pricing on to individual customers.

A direct DHL contract typically nets a 30–40% discount, while an agent like Tosome Logis can secure discounts of up to 80%. That means the same shipment can go out for roughly half the price.

Understanding Volumetric (Dim) Weight

Couriers charge based on whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight. If a box is large but its contents are light, the volumetric weight will be higher — pushing your cost above what you'd expect from the actual weight alone.

Volumetric weight = length(cm) × width(cm) × height(cm) ÷ 5,000
Example: 40 × 30 × 20cm = 24,000 ÷ 5,000 = 4.8kg
If the actual weight is 2kg, you'll still be charged based on 4.8kg
