Alibaba B2B Shipping to Korea
How to Import Directly Through a Freight Forwarder

Negotiated with a factory on Alibaba.com and already paid — but now shipping feels like a wall you can't get past? Routing through a parcel forwarder actually costs you more. Understand the structure, ship directly through a freight forwarder instead, and you'll save money and avoid customs headaches.

1. Alibaba.com vs. 1688 — What's the Difference?

Both platforms belong to the Alibaba Group, but they serve different purposes — and that has a direct effect on how you should approach B2B importing.

Alibaba.com1688
Primary customersOverseas buyers (B2B export-oriented)Domestic Chinese wholesale buyers
LanguageEnglish/Korean supportedChinese
MOQMedium to largeSmall to large
PaymentTrade Assurance (secure payment)Alipay (requires a Chinese bank account)
Shipping termsIncoterms negotiable — EXW, FOB, DAP, etc.Typically routed through a parcel forwarder
💡 Alibaba.com factories often have export experience, which makes it possible to hand cargo directly to a freight forwarder under FOB or EXW terms. That's a much better setup for direct freight forwarding than 1688.

2. How to Import Directly Through a Freight Forwarder — No Parcel Forwarder Needed

Routing Alibaba B2B volume through a parcel forwarder actually works against you. There are three reasons why.

  • Parcel forwarder warehousing and repackaging fees add up. The more boxes you have, the more it costs.
  • You'll hit the personal import de minimis (roughly $150). B2B volume needs to be declared as a formal business import.
  • Certificate of origin and HS code handling aren't available. You need a freight forwarder to actually claim FTA duty benefits.

Here's how the direct freight forwarding process works.

3. Parcel Forwarder vs. Direct Freight Forwarding — Compared

Here's a side-by-side comparison of both methods. For B2B volume, the answer is clear.

Parcel ForwarderDirect Freight Forwarding
Best forSmall volume (1–5 boxes)Medium to large volume (0.5 CBM+)
Import entityIndividual (simplified clearance)Business (formal import declaration)
Personal de minimis$150 capNo cap
Cost structureForwarder fee + warehousing + freight (itemized separately)All-in quote (no hidden extras)
Customs handlingBasic handling by the parcel forwarder (limited HS code review)HS code pre-check + duty optimization
FTA duty benefitsNot availableAvailable via CO issuance
Origin markingDifficult to verifyChecked and corrected before shipping
Lead time (China → Incheon)7–14 days (including parcel forwarder transit)~5–7 days by ocean LCL (from Shanghai)
⚠️ If you judge purely by the headline freight rate, you'll lose out on CFS fees, customs fees, and inland transport costs. Always compare on a total (all-in) cost basis.
