Shipping from Korea to the USA 2026
Sea, Air, DDP & the Customs Process

Shipping from Korea to the USA trips up a lot of first-timers — the transit is long, and CBP (US Customs) procedures are exacting. Here's a 2026 reference covering LCL sea freight vs. air vs. DDP terms, lead times to major US ports of entry, and customs precautions.

3. The 7-Step USA Export Process

01
Choose a forwarder & decide incoterms
Coordinate with your buyer on which incoterm — EXW, FOB, DAP, or DDP — to use.
DAP: delivered to a specified location, tariff paid by importer DDP: exporter covers tariff, clearance, and delivery (maximum convenience for buyer)
02
Cargo pickup & packing
Your forwarder picks up cargo from the origin point. Wood packaging (pallets, crates) requires ISPM 15 fumigation treatment.
03
Export declaration (EEI)
Exports to the USA require filing Electronic Export Information (EEI) through AES (Automated Export System). Your forwarder handles this on your behalf.
EEI filing is mandatory for cargo valued over $2,500
04
Loading & sea/air transit
Departs from Busan Port or Incheon International Airport. A B/L or AWB is issued.
05
Pre-arrival security filing (ISF)
Ocean cargo requires an ISF (Importer Security Filing) submitted to CBP 24 hours before US arrival. Your forwarder handles this under DDP terms.
06
US customs clearance (CBP)
Import declaration is filed with US Customs and Border Protection (CBP). If documents check out, clearance typically completes in 1-2 days.
FDA-regulated items (food, cosmetics, medical devices): a separate pre-filing is required CPSC-regulated items (children's products, electrical goods): certification must be ready
07
Last-mile delivery
Delivered to the buyer's specified address after clearance. Under DDP terms, your forwarder handles delivery as part of the package.

4. What Is Export Under DDP Terms? — When You Need It

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) is an incoterm where the exporter takes on full responsibility for local tariffs, customs clearance, and last-mile delivery.

✓
When your buyer has no US customs experience
Individual or small-business buyers often find CBP clearance procedures unfamiliar. DDP removes that burden from the importer entirely.
✓
Items with complex regulations like FDA filing
Items requiring FDA pre-notification — food, cosmetics, medical devices — are safer when your forwarder handles the whole process under DDP.
✓
B2C sales or Amazon FBA delivery
For direct delivery to an Amazon FBA warehouse, it's standard practice to hand off clearance and delivery to your forwarder under DDP terms.

About 12-15 days to LA (Long Beach), 22-26 days to New York/New Jersey (from Busan, ocean transit only). Plan on roughly 20-30 days total for LCL, from factory pickup through delivery in the USA.
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) is an incoterm where the exporter covers all costs — local tariffs, customs clearance, and last-mile delivery — and delivers to the importer's specified location. It's advantageous when the buyer has no customs experience or faces complex regulations like FDA filing.
The basic documents are a B/L (or AWB), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and HS code. Ocean cargo requires an ISF filing 24 hours before arrival, and an EEI filing is required for export. Food and cosmetics need FDA filings, and wood packaging requires ISPM 15 fumigation certification.
Under DAP terms, the importer (buyer) is responsible for US tariffs. Under DDP terms, the exporter covers US tariffs as well. Items eligible under the Korea-US FTA can access the agreed tariff rate with proof of origin.
