How to Import from China to Korea 2026
Forwarding, Customs, FTA & Port Strategy

If you're sourcing from Alibaba, 1688, Shenzhen, or Guangzhou factories for the first time, the part that trips people up most is the import process and cost structure for shipping from China to Korea. Here's a single, practical 2026 reference covering how to choose between LCL and FCL, customs clearance, FTA tariff benefits, and lead times by port.

1. Three Ways to Import from China — Parcel Forwarding vs. Freight Forwarding vs. Direct Courier

The right method depends on cargo volume and purpose. For B2B business imports, freight forwarding is almost always the better choice on both cost and customs grounds.

MethodBest ForImporter TypeCost StructureFTA Tariff Benefit
Parcel forwarding agent1-5 boxes, small quantityIndividual (simplified clearance)Agent fee + freight (itemized separately)Not available
Freight forwarding (LCL/FCL)0.5+ CBM, medium-large volumeBusiness (formal import declaration)All-in quote availableAvailable with a CO
Direct courierSmall quantity, urgent cargoIndividuals and businessesDHL/FedEx freight ratesNot available
💡 B2B volume exceeding the personal customs clearance limit (approx. $150) requires a formal business import declaration. Routing through a parcel-forwarding agent makes it hard to confirm HS codes or handle origin labeling, and you won't be eligible for FTA tariff benefits either.

02
Factory pickup — handled by a local partner
Your forwarder's local partner in China picks up cargo from the factory or warehouse and moves it to the CFS warehouse.
EXW terms: pickup directly from the factory door FOB terms: factory delivers to the designated port, forwarder takes over from there
03
Origin labeling & document check
Before departure, check for missing or incorrect origin labeling. If you need an FTA CO, this is when you apply for it.
04
Loading — CFS consolidation, then container loading
LCL cargo is consolidated with other shippers' freight; FCL is loaded into a dedicated container, then the vessel departs.
05
Ocean transit — arrival at Incheon Port
A B/L (Bill of Lading) is issued, and cargo arrives at Incheon Port within 2-5 days depending on the departure port.
06
Import customs clearance
A customs broker files the import declaration. If documents check out, clearance completes within 1-2 days.
Items with separate requirements (KC certification, MFDS approval, etc.) take longer Submitting an FTA CO applies tariff savings
07
Inland transportation & final delivery
Delivered to the designated address after clearance. Release within the free bonded-warehouse period (7 days with Tosome) means no extra storage fee.

5. FTA Tariff Benefits & Common Clearance Blockers

Using the Korea-China FTA, submitting a Certificate of Origin (CO) alone can bring your tariff down to anywhere from 0% to a few percent. HS code errors, missing origin labeling, and lack of KC certification are the most common causes of delay or return shipment during China import clearance. See our China Import Customs Clearance Guide for the full CO process and top 5 clearance blockers.
