Power banks, laptop batteries, e-scooter batteries, industrial batteries. Packing group varies by Wh rating.

Our Process

Tosome Logis
handles everything

One wrong document or packaging step can mean your shipment is returned or seized. Our specialists handle it from start to finish.

01
Cargo intake & dangerous goods classification
We accurately classify the UN Class and packing group based on item name, MSDS, and Wh rating.
02
Professional packing with UN-certified materials
Packaging that has passed IATA's UN38.3 test, with compliant labeling attached.
03
Complete dangerous goods declaration & documentation
We prepare all documents required for customs and airline submission, including the DG Declaration.
04
Airline pre-approval & export customs clearance
We select an airline that accepts the dangerous goods, secure pre-approval, and handle export customs clearance.
